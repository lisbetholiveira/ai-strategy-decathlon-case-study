# Detailed Analysis — AI Opportunities for Decathlon

This document expands the six strategic opportunity areas identified in the original IPAM academic project.

## 1. Short term — Personalisation of the customer experience

### Business challenge
Decathlon serves very different customer segments, from beginners and families to experienced athletes. Generic communication risks losing relevance across such a broad customer base.

### AI opportunity
Use customer history, preferences, behaviour and contextual information to tailor recommendations and communications across online and offline channels.

### Example explored in the project
A customer in Matosinhos who regularly buys energy bars and running equipment could receive recommendations for trail shoes adapted to the local context or invitations to nearby running events rather than generic marketing.

### Expected value
**For the customer**
- More relevant recommendations
- Faster product choice
- More personalised experience

**For the business**
- Greater relevance of customer communications
- Stronger cross-channel experience
- Better alignment between customer needs and product discovery

---

## 2. Short term — Stock and logistics optimisation

### Business challenge
Sports retail demand is highly sensitive to seasonality, weather and local events.

### AI opportunity
Use predictive demand-forecasting models that combine sales history with external variables such as weather patterns and local events.

### Examples explored in the project
- Rain increasing demand for waterproof products
- Heatwaves increasing demand for camping equipment
- Local sporting events affecting demand patterns

### Expected value
**For Decathlon**
- Better planning
- Greater operational efficiency
- Potential cost reduction

**For customers**
- Better product availability
- Less waiting
- Improved shopping experience

---

## 3. Short term — Intelligent virtual assistants

### Business challenge
Traditional customer-service bots are often limited to transactional questions and predefined flows.

### AI opportunity
Use generative AI and natural-language processing to understand context and intent, enabling a virtual assistant to act more like a digital sports adviser.

### Scenario explored in the project
A customer asks for equipment advice for walking the Camino de Santiago from Chaves in October. The assistant interprets the route and season, combines that context with product characteristics and available stock, and recommends suitable footwear, socks and a backpack with direct product links.

### Expected value
**For Decathlon**
- Greater service efficiency
- Reduced operational workload
- 24/7 availability

**For customers**
- Immediate responses
- Continuous support
- Greater convenience

---

# Long-term transformation

## 4. Personalised digital coach

### Strategic concept
AI evolves from a shopping assistant into a continuous coaching service connected to a customer's goals, activity history and sporting journey.

### Potential capabilities
- Training plans
- Weekly objectives
- Recovery guidance
- Equipment recommendations
- Adaptive recommendations as performance changes

### Scenario explored in the project
A customer preparing for a first half-marathon receives a 12-week training plan. The plan adapts according to training completion and fatigue indicators.

### Potential value for Decathlon
- Increased loyalty
- New digital services
- Recurring revenue opportunities

### Potential value for customers
- Personalised guidance
- Greater motivation
- Better outcomes

---

## 5. Wearables and performance monitoring

### Strategic concept
Integrate connected-device data with AI to create more adaptive sports and wellbeing experiences.

### Data considered in the scenario
- Activity and training data
- Resting heart rate
- Sleep quality
- Recovery indicators
- Training load

### Scenario explored in the project
A wearable-connected experience detects signs of poorer recovery and adapts recommendations, such as reducing training intensity or increasing recovery time.

### Potential value for Decathlon
- Deeper customer understanding
- Advanced personalisation
- Competitive differentiation

### Potential value for customers
- More personalised experience
- Improved performance support
- Greater safety awareness

> The original project presents these as future scenarios, not as currently implemented Decathlon capabilities.

---

## 6. Phygital stores

### Strategic concept
Merge the physical and digital experience so that the customer's context, preferences and digital journey continue inside the store.

### Technologies explored
- Computer vision
- AI-assisted recommendations
- Augmented-reality mirrors
- Smart fitting experiences
- Frictionless checkout concepts

### Scenario explored in the project
A customer could visualise the fit of a surf suit through an augmented-reality experience and receive context-aware recommendations. Computer-vision systems could also support checkout-free shopping concepts.

### Strategic value
- More personalised in-store interactions
- Reduced purchase friction
- Better integration between online and offline journeys
- Potential reduction in unsuitable product choices and exchanges

# Transformation logic

The six opportunities can be understood as a progression:

| Stage | AI role | Business effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Optimise | Improve forecasting, stock and service efficiency |
| 2 | Personalise | Make recommendations and interactions more relevant |
| 3 | Engage | Build an ongoing relationship through coaching and connected services |
| 4 | Transform | Create integrated phygital experiences and new service models |

The project's strategic thesis is that the largest long-term opportunity lies in moving beyond isolated AI use cases toward a connected ecosystem spanning products, digital services, data and physical retail.

# Methodological note

The original project used ChatGPT and Gemini for brainstorming and structuring. Ideas were critically reviewed rather than treated as verified facts. This portfolio case study therefore distinguishes between current-state observations, strategic opportunities and speculative future scenarios.
